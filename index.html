Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if I change my WiFi password?

A: Just update it on our dashboard in seconds. No need to reprint QR codes or re-configure the stands. It syncs instantly.

Q: Is it safe for my Google Business Profile?

A: Yes, absolutely. We use standard URL redirection which is fully compliant with Google's terms. We simply make the process faster for the customer.

Q: Do I need to buy the hardware again efficiently?

About Us

Bridging the Gap Between Hospitality & Technology.

Our Story

TapFlow Solutions was born from a simple observation: Restaurants in Dhaka make amazing food, but they struggle to get the online recognition they deserve. We saw waiters awkwardly asking for reviews and customers frustrated with typing long WiFi passwords. We knew there had to be a smarter way.

Who We Are

We are a Dhaka-based technology agency specialized in Restaurant Automation. We don't just sell hardware; we provide a digital ecosystem. From NFC-powered Review Systems to Smart WiFi Gateways, our mission is to turn every table into a digital touchpoint.

Our Mission

To empower 10,000+ restaurants in Bangladesh with affordable, high-tech automation tools that increase revenue and customer loyalty.

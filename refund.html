REFUND, RETURN & CANCELLATION POLICY

Last Updated: January 2026

1. ADVANCE PAYMENTS (NON-REFUNDABLE)

The 50% Advance Payment required to confirm a work order is strictly Non-Refundable. This amount is immediately utilized to procure raw materials (NFC chips, custom printing) and reserve server slots.

2. HARDWARE DEFECTS & REPLACEMENT

Coverage: If any NFC Stand or Sticker is found technically defective (chip not readable) within 7 days of delivery, we will replace it Free of Cost.

Exclusion: This warranty does NOT cover physical breakage, water damage, or scratches caused after delivery.

Shipping: For returns outside Dhaka, the client must bear the shipping cost to send the defective unit to our office. TapFlow Solutions will pay for shipping the new replacement unit.

3. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION

Clients can cancel their "Monthly Management" subscription at any time. The service will continue until the end of the current billing cycle. However, fees already paid for the active month are non-refundable.

4. SERVICE DOWNTIME & CREDITS

In the rare event that our server is down for more than 48 consecutive hours due to our internal technical fault, we will issue a pro-rated credit to your next month's bill.

Exception (Force Majeure): We are NOT liable for downtime caused by nationwide internet blackouts, government restrictions, or ISP failures beyond our control. No refunds will be issued in such cases.

5. REFUND TIMELINE

In exceptional cases where a refund is approved by management, the amount will be processed within 7-10 working days via the original payment method (Bank/Mobile Wallet).

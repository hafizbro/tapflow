PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to TapFlow Solutions ("Company", "We", "Us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you use our NFC/QR-based services or connect to our partner WiFi networks.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Connect to a Free WiFi network managed by our system.
  • Submit feedback through our digital forms.

Data Points May Include: Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, Device Information (OS, Browser Type), Date and Time of Visit.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the collected data for specific business purposes:

  • Connectivity: To authenticate your device and grant access to the secured WiFi network.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage trends and improve the stability of our system.
  • Partner Marketing: Your contact details are shared exclusively with the specific restaurant/business you visited. They may use this information to send you loyalty rewards, discounts, or updates.

4. WILL YOUR INFORMATION BE SHARED?

We operate under a strict No-Sell Policy. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers. Your data is strictly shared only with the business establishment where you physically used the service.

5. DATA SECURITY

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, please also remember that the internet itself is not 100% secure. Although we will do our best to protect your personal information, transmission of personal information to and from our services is at your own risk.

6. POLICY UPDATES

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Revised" date and will be effective as soon as it is accessible.
